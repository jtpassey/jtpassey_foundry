# Sprint 1 Plan

**Menu:** Build for DVL, discovery and design

**Teammates:** A teammate

**Goal:** Map out UI/UX improvements for DVL's software, with the rationale behind them.

**My part:** Set up meetings with end users, interview them, and identify their jobs to be done.

**Who will see it:** The DVL sales team, who will use the software for their daily work, and DVL's product owner.

**Why this:** DVL's product owner already has an alpha product and is a confident builder with AI. He needs a team to make it easy to use, simplify a process, and multiply the amount of work his teams can do. Understanding how people actually interact with the tools will lead to the strongest build.

**What done looks like:** A written document, committed in this repo, that lists all the UI/UX recommendations and the rationale behind each one. The rationale is founded on real user interviews with DVL end users.

**Predicted difficulty:** 3
